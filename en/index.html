How to Write Better Prompts

Learn how to give clear instructions to AI to get more useful responses.

Read Guide ＋

What Is a Prompt?

A prompt is an instruction or question you give an AI tool to explain what you need.

How Can You Write a Better Prompt?

  1. Explain what you need.
  2. Provide relevant context.
  3. Describe your preferred response format.
  4. Add important details.

Practical Example

Instead of writing:

Explain programming.

Try a more detailed instruction:

Explain object-oriented programming in simple terms. Include a Java example and organize the explanation by concept.

Tip: If the answer is not what you expected, ask the AI to explain it differently.

How to Study with Artificial Intelligence

Use AI tools to organize notes, understand concepts, and prepare for exams.

Read Guide ＋

How Can AI Help You?

AI can support your learning by helping you understand topics, organize information, and practice what you have learned.

Ways to Use AI for Studying

  1. Summarize your notes to identify key ideas.
  2. Ask for explanations of difficult concepts.
  3. Generate practice questions about a topic.
  4. Create a study plan organized by subject.

Practical Example

I'm studying databases. Explain the differences between primary keys and foreign keys. Include simple SQL table examples and five practice questions.

Tip: Try solving exercises yourself before checking the answers. Verify information using your class materials.

What Can You Create?

Learn how to turn your ideas into organized, visually appealing presentations.

Which Tools Can You Use?

Platforms such as Gamma and Canva offer features that help create presentations from prompts and content.

Steps to Create a Presentation

  1. Define the topic and goal.
  2. Specify how many slides you need.
  3. Describe your target audience.
  4. Generate an initial draft.
  5. Review the text, images, and design.

Example Prompt

Create a six-slide presentation about the importance of cybersecurity for university students. Use short titles, simple explanations, and everyday examples.

Tip: Use AI as a starting point. Check the facts and adapt the design to your presentation needs.

Learn how to find information, explore sources, and verify the facts you discover.

Read Guide ＋

How Can AI Support Research?

AI can help you explore related topics, understand complex information, and organize research findings.

Steps for Better Research

  1. Clearly define your research topic.
  2. Ask specific questions.
  3. Use tools that provide sources and references.
  4. Read the original sources.
  5. Compare information before using it.

Example Prompt

Research the main benefits of solar energy. Organize the information by topic and include reliable sources to verify the facts.

Tip: Do not assume every AI answer is correct. Verify references, dates, and information using reliable sources.

PRODUCTIVITY

How to Organize Tasks and Projects with AI

Use AI to plan activities, set priorities, and manage your time.

Read Guide ＋

How Can AI Help?

AI can help you divide large projects into smaller tasks, create schedules, and organize activities by priority.

Steps to Organize a Project

  1. Describe the project.
  2. Provide the deadline.
  3. Ask for a list of tasks.
  4. Organize tasks by priority.
  5. Review progress and update your plan.

Example Prompt

Help me organize a university project due in three weeks. Divide the work into tasks, set priorities, and create a weekly schedule.

Tip: Use your plan as a guide and adjust it based on your availability and actual progress.
