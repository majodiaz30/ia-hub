Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

At IA Hub, we respect the privacy of our visitors. This policy explains what information is used when browsing our artificial intelligence tools directory and how your preferences are stored.

1. What Is IA Hub?

IA Hub is an informational directory that allows users to discover, explore, and learn about different artificial intelligence tools. Our goal is to make these platforms easier to access by providing information and links to their official websites.

Currently, you do not need to create an account or register to use the directory's features.

2. Information We Store

IA Hub currently does not have registration, login, or subscription forms that request personal information from visitors.

However, some preferences are stored directly in your browser using localStorage technology.

3. Storing Favorites

When you mark a tool as a favorite, IA Hub uses localStorage to save that preference in your device's browser.

  • You do not need to provide your name or email address.
  • Favorites remain saved in the browser you use.
  • Preferences are not automatically synchronized between devices.
  • You can remove favorites directly from the website.
  • Clearing your browser's stored data may remove your favorites.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

IA Hub currently uses localStorage to save preferences such as favorites. This technology is different from cookies.

We currently do not have Google Analytics or Google AdSense advertising installed on the website.

Some external services used to display website resources, such as fonts or icons, may receive requests from your browser and process technical information according to their own policies.

5. Links to External Websites

IA Hub contains links to third-party artificial intelligence platforms.

When you follow one of these links, you visit an external website that has its own terms of use and privacy policy.

IA Hub does not control the privacy practices or information processing activities of these platforms. We recommend reviewing their policies before using them.

6. Security and Hosting Services

IA Hub is hosted using third-party services. These providers may process technical information necessary to deliver, protect, and keep the website available, according to their own policies.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when new features, tools, or services are added to the website.

Any changes will be published on this page, along with the date of the latest update.

8. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please visit our Contact page.
