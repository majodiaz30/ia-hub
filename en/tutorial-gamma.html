INTRODUCTION

What is Gamma?

Gamma is an AI-powered platform that helps users create presentations, documents and websites from written instructions.

Instead of designing every slide manually, you can describe your topic and let Gamma suggest an initial structure with text and visual elements that you can customize.

PRACTICAL TUTORIAL

Create your first presentation

Follow these steps to generate a presentation using Gamma.

01

Visit Gamma

Go to gamma.app and create an account or sign in. You can explore the features available in its free plan.

02

Create a new project

Look for the option to create content with AI. Choose a presentation and select the option to generate content from an idea. Menu names may vary.

03

Describe your topic

Explain what your presentation should cover, who your audience is and what information you want to include.

04

Customize the outline

Review the suggested structure, adjust the sections and choose a visual style that matches your presentation.

05

Generate and review your slides

Let Gamma generate the first draft. Then review the text, verify important facts and edit any images or sections that need improvement.

06

Share your presentation

Use the available options to share your work through a link or export it to a supported format, depending on your plan.

RECOMMENDATIONS

Tips for better results

Define your audience

Specify whether your presentation is for students, teachers or professionals.

Choose the length

Tell Gamma how many slides you need and which topics to include.

Verify the information

Double-check dates, numbers and important claims before presenting.

Customize the result

Edit the text, colors and visual elements to match your ideas.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about Gamma

Is Gamma free?

Gamma offers a free plan with usage limits and selected features. Paid plans are also available.

Can I edit my presentations?

Yes. You can edit the content, rearrange sections and customize different design elements.

Can I export to PowerPoint?

Gamma provides export options, including PowerPoint. Some features and limits depend on your plan.

Does Gamma do everything automatically?

Gamma can create an initial draft, but you should review the facts, correct possible errors and adapt the presentation to your needs.
